# Features

Desktop workflows in Curio, from filesystem-first storage and browser capture to color browsing, smart folders, viewers, tools and workspace customization.

## Import

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **Libraries & storage**

    ---

    Create `.curio` libraries, mix copied managed files (`files/` hard-link mirror) with in-place indexed locations, transfer assets between libraries, run multiple windows and schedule backups.

    [Create and manage libraries](libraries/manage.md) · [Storage modes](libraries/storage.md) · [Locations](libraries/locations.md) · [Transfers](libraries/transfers.md) · [Multiple windows](libraries/windows.md) · [Back up & restore](libraries/backup.md)

-   **Import, capture & export**

    ---

    Drop files and folders, paste clipboard images or URL lists, capture from the web with **Send to Curio**, automate watched folders with **Auto-Import**, import Eagle `.library` folders and export files or sidecars.

    [Files & clipboard](import/files.md) · [Links & downloads](import/links.md) · [Browser capture](import/browser.md) · [Auto-Import](import/auto.md) · [Import from Eagle](import/eagle.md) · [Export](import/export.md)

## Organize

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **Folders, tags & Inspector**

    ---

    File assets into multiple folders, pin favorite folders and locations, manage tags and tag groups, rate, favorite or mark assets explicit in the Inspector and drag-and-drop across windows and external apps.

    [Folders & pinned items](organize/folders.md) · [Tags & tag groups](organize/tags.md) · [Inspector](organize/inspector.md) · [File operations & trash](organize/file-actions.md) · [Drag & drop](organize/drag-drop.md)

-   **Metadata, grouping & actions**

    ---

    Define typed custom fields, enable built-in and plugin file readers (EXIF, GPS, audio/video, fonts), combine RAW+JPEG, Live Photos and companions into one asset and automate repeated edits with reusable actions.

    [Custom fields](organize/fields.md) · [Metadata readers](organize/metadata-readers.md) · [Grouped files](organize/grouped-files.md) · [Reusable actions](organize/actions.md)

</div>

## Browse & view

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **Browse, search & filter**

    ---

    Browse quick views and folder trees in Grid, Masonry, Justified, List or **Spectrum** color space, combine prefix search with the [Query language](../api/query/syntax.md), save filter presets and build nested Smart Folders with virtual subfolders.

    [Browse scopes](browse/scopes.md) · [Views & Group by](browse/views.md) · [Spectrum](browse/spectrum.md) · [Search](search/text.md) · [Filters & presets](search/filters.md) · [Smart folders](search/smart-folders.md)

-   **Viewers & formats**

    ---

    Inspect images, animated media, video with subtitles, audio waveforms, Markdown, code, web links and fonts, plus optional plugins for EPUB/PDF/CBZ books, 3D models and AI generation metadata.

    [Supported formats](viewer/formats.md) · [Viewer navigation](viewer/navigation.md) · [Images](viewer/images.md) · [Video & audio](viewer/media.md) · [Text, code & links](viewer/text-links.md) · [Fonts](viewer/fonts.md) · [Reader](viewer/reader.md) · [3D models](viewer/models.md) · [AI metadata](viewer/ai.md)

-   **Built-in tools**

    ---

    Rename selections with token rules and undo, find exact and perceptual duplicates across five comparison modes and compare two pictures side by side, with a divider, overlay or pixel difference view.

    [Batch rename](tools/rename.md) · [Find duplicates](tools/duplicates.md) · [Compare pictures](tools/compare.md)

</div>
