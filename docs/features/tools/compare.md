# Compare

**Compare** opens a dedicated window to inspect two pictures side by side or overlaid.

## Select items

- Select two pictures in Curio and choose **Compare** from the **Item menu**. You can also open **Compare** from the toolbar or Main menu.
- Drag pictures from the grid onto the Compare window to load or swap either side:
  - Drop **two pictures** at once to replace both side **A** and side **B**.
  - Drop **one picture** onto the left half to replace side **A**, or onto the right half to replace side **B** (or fill whichever side is empty).

## Modes

Switch comparison modes in the top segmented bar:

| Mode | Visual behavior |
|---|---|
| **Slider** *(default)* | Overlays A and B with a draggable split divider so you can wipe across fine detail. |
| **Side by side** | Displays A and B next to each other with synchronized zoom and pan. |
| **Overlay** | Stacks A and B with an opacity slider to spot subtle shifts or color changes. |
| **Difference** | Highlights pixel-level differences between A and B so edits or compression artifacts stand out. |

Compare loads 2048 px previews so large RAWs and high-resolution pictures zoom without lag.

## Zoom & pan

- Zoom and pan both pictures in lockstep to inspect corners and fine textures in alignment. Toggle **Link views** in the viewer bar if you want to pan independently.
- Rate side **A** or side **B** in the footer without losing your zoom or pan position. Ratings save to the library as you click.

## Metadata

Click **Properties** in the footer to open a side-by-side table of both assets with differing rows highlighted and a difference count:

- **Name**, **Dimensions** (marking which side has more pixels), **Megapixels**, **File size**, **Format**, **Rating**, **Tags**, **Folders** and **Added**.
